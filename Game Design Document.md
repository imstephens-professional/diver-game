## 1. Introduction
### 1.1 Game Title
"Diver: Ruins Resurfaced" 
### 1.2 Game Pitch
“Diver: Ruins Resurfaced” is a platformer where you play as a cave diver who gets separated from their friends and stumbles upon ancient ruins. 
You’ll have to face challenges to find your friends and discover the secrets hidden in the ruins. With exciting gameplay, a captivating storyline, and 
incredible graphics “Diver: Ruins Resurfaced” offers an adventure like no other. Can you locate your diving buddies and unveil the ancient secrets hidden within the 
ruins? Dive right in and embark on a riveting journey!

## 5. Interface
### 5.1 Visual System
**Main Menu**: It will show before gameplay begins prompting the player to “Start”. 

**Difficulty Menu**: It will be available before gameplay and after the Main Menu. It will prompt the user to select between Easy Mode or Hard Mode. 
### 5.2 Control System
**Movement**: All based on the keyboard. The player will use the up (^), down (V), left (<), and right (>) keys to move around the map. The grappling hook will be triggered by the key “Z”. 
### 5.3 Audio
**Sound Effects**: There will be movement and environmental sound effects. 

## 6. Artificial Intelligence
### 6.1 Enemy AI
The Wanderer enemy will remain on a platform and will follow the player if they land on their platform. The Wanderer will be slower than the player character. The Wanderer can only move left or right on the platform; it cannot move up or down, nor can it leave the platform. 

## 7. Technical
### 7.1 Target Hardware
The game will be specifically designed to be played through a browser. The game will be uploaded and available as a web-based application (html). The game will be designed to be controlled via the keyboard during gameplay.  
### 7.2 Development Hardware & Software
The game will be developed on HP Pavilion 15, Macbook Pro, and Microsoft Surface Laptop based on the developer's current available hardware. The software will be developed using the Pico-8 game engine.
### 7.3 Network Requirements
Any signal - public or private - above 2G. 

## 8. Game Art
No outside assets are necessary. All assets will be developed in the Pico-8 system.
