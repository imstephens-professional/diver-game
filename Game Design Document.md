### 1.2 Game Pitch
“Diver: Ruins Resurfaced” is a platformer where you play as a cave diver who gets separated from their friends and stumbles upon ancient ruins. 
You’ll have to face challenges to find your friends and discover the secrets hidden in the ruins. With exciting gameplay, a captivating storyline, and 
incredible graphics “Diver: Ruins Resurfaced” offers an adventure like no other. Can you locate your diving buddies and unveil the ancient secrets hidden within the 
ruins? Dive right in and embark on a riveting journey!
